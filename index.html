Zhuofei Lu

Postdoctoral Researcher in Sociology · University of Oxford

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Sociology and course convenor at the School of Global and Area Studies, University of Oxford, working on the ‘GenTime’ project. My current research sits at the intersection of computational social science and policy evaluation — drawing on survey, time-diary, biomarker, and large text-corpus data to study work, well-being, and inequality. Three threads are active right now: public acceptance of generative AI across Europe, a century of occupational gender-typing surfaced through diachronic word embeddings, and the welfare consequences of UK flexible-working legislation.

Methodologically I work with sequence analysis, causal inference, machine learning, and natural language processing — applied to large-scale longitudinal household panel data, time-diary data, biomarker data, and historical text corpora. I serve on the editorial boards of Work, Employment & Society and Social Science Research, and as Associate Editor of Humanities and Social Sciences Communications.
