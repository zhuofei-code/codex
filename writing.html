The Sociology of Human-Centred AI

The Renaissance asked what it meant to place human dignity, reason, creativity, and beauty at the centre of a changing world. The age of AI asks us to face that question again. These essays explore how artificial intelligence is reshaping work, time, culture, knowledge, and everyday life — bringing together sociology, art, literature, and Renaissance humanism. Not about technology itself, but about the social worlds we build around it, and whether they can remain worthy of human beings.
